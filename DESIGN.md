# DESIGN.md: Astrolabe

How Astrolabe's surfaces (the landing page in `site/` and anything built next to it) should look and feel. Agents read this before building UI. The token values live in `site/tokens.json`, and CI fails the landing page if any rendered value is off-token, so this file and that one must agree.

## Overview

A calm daytime sky with precise instruments floating in it. The motif is the astrolabe itself, a dial for reading the sky: split-flap readouts, a radar sweep behind the scan, and a line-art dial with crosshairs and an off-center rete. Big airy blue hero, soft drifting clouds, frosted-glass cards that show real product output, then clean white sections with a bento grid. Technical, honest and a little playful. One electric accent (lime) against sky blue and near-black ink.

The product checks design-token fidelity, so the site must itself be 100% on-token. Never show fake stats, testimonials or client logos; every number on the page is real CLI output or a true fact about the tool.

## Colors

### Brand & Accent
- `sky.900` `#0a3a8c`, `sky.700` `#1462d6`, `sky.500` `#3a8cf0`, `sky.300` `#9cc8fb`: the sky. Hero and closing gradients run 900 → 700 → 500 → 300 top to bottom.
- `sky.100` `#e7f2ff`: pale tint for inline chips and stripes.
- `lime` `#d4fa45`: the only accent. Primary buttons, step numbers, the scan beam, selection, focus halo. Text on lime is always `ink`.

### Surface
- `paper` `#ffffff`: page background and cards.
- `mist` `#f2f4f8`: quiet panels, the "How it works" band, code chips.
- Glass on sky: `glass` `#ffffffa6` with `glass-line` `#ffffffcc` border, `glass-strong` `#ffffffd9` for the scrolled nav, `glass-dark` `#0b10208c` for secondary buttons on sky.

### Text
- `ink` `#0b1020` (never pure black), `ink-2` `#262d3d`, `muted` `#5b6476` for body copy and labels, `subtle` `#8b93a5` only for large softened headline halves and decoration.
- On sky: `paper` for body text, `on-sky` `#ffffffd1` only for large display text. On ink: `on-ink` `#ffffffa3`.

### Semantic
Status colors mirror the CLI: `amber` `#f59e0b` near-miss, `red` `#ef4444` off-system, `green` `#22c55e` on-token, each with a `-soft` background (`#fff4dc`, `#fde8e8`, `#e3f8ea`). Always pair color with a text label ("near-miss", "off-system").

## Typography

### Font Family
- `Inter Tight` (400, 500, 600) for everything readable.
- `JetBrains Mono` (400, 500) for code, CLI output, eyebrows, nav links and button labels.

Load both from Google Fonts with plain `<link>` tags. Do not use `next/font`, because it renames the families and the CI token check matches family names.

### Hierarchy
| Role | Size | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display (hero, closing) | 72 / 56 / 40px by breakpoint | 500 | tracking -0.045em, line-height 1.02; second line 400 in a softer color |
| Section heading | 56 / 40 / 32px | 500 | tracking -0.04em, the trailing clause in `subtle` |
| Card title | 20–24px | 500 | tracking -0.02em |
| Big number | 32–56px | 500 | rolls in with the Counter |
| Body | 16–18px | 400 | `muted`, line-height 1.5–1.55, max ~65ch |
| Small / label | 13–14px | 400 | |
| Eyebrow, nav, buttons | 11–12px mono | 500 | uppercase, tracking 0.1–0.14em, eyebrow has a dot before it |

### Principles
- Only sizes from the scale: 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24, 32, 40, 56, 72. Change sizes at breakpoints, never with `clamp()`, because fluid values produce off-token pixels.
- Weights 400, 500 and 600 only. `<b>` and `<strong>` are 600.
- Numbers that compare or animate use tabular figures.
- Sentence case for headings and buttons. Balance headings with `text-wrap: balance`.

## Layout

### Spacing System
2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96, 128, 160, 200px (`--s-1` … `--s17`). Nothing else for margin, padding or gap.

### Grid & Container
Container max 1200px with 24px gutters (16px under 760px). Bento grids use CSS grid with unequal tracks (for example `1.05fr 1.35fr 1.2fr`, or 4 columns with a 2-span tile). Avoid three equal feature cards.

### Whitespace Philosophy
Generous. Sections breathe at 128px vertical padding (80px on mobile). Section heads sit 80px above their content.

### Responsive Strategy
Breakpoints at 1100, 860 and 760px. Under 760px everything collapses to one column, and the hero carousel uses smaller cards on the same ring. There's no horizontal page scroll at any width.

## Elevation & Depth
- Shadows are tinted toward the sky (`rgba(10, 58, 140, …)`) or ink, never neutral black, and never a colored glow under buttons.
- Glass: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(150%)`, a 1px light border, an inset top highlight, and a radial glare that follows the pointer.

### Decorative Depth
Four cloud layers (WebP, generated by `site/tools/clouds.py`) drift at different speeds and shift with the pointer and scroll. The front layer passes in front of the carousel. Clouds pause off-screen, stop for reduced motion, and stop when the visitor pauses motion.

## Shapes

### Border Radius Scale
6, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32px, plus `999px` for pills. Nested radii shrink inward: a 24px card holds 16px panels holding 12px controls.

### Photography Geometry
No stock photos. Imagery is generated (clouds) or real product output (reports, CLI, PR comments).

## Components

### Buttons
- Primary: lime pill, mono uppercase 12px label, with an ink circle holding an arrow that rotates 45° on hover.
- Secondary on sky: `glass-dark` pill with a 1px `ink-line` border.
- Dark: ink pill with a lime arrow circle.
- Hover lifts 2px; press scales to 0.98.

### Cards & Containers
- Bento cards: 24px radius, 24px padding. Each has a cursor-following edge light, a spotlight and a slight tilt (adapted from Magic Bento). Variants: sky, mist, lime, ink.
- Glass cards in the hero: tilt toward the pointer with a moving glare (adapted from Tilted Card).

### Inputs & Forms
48px tall, 12px radius, `mist` fill, 1px `line` border, 16px mono text. Labels are present, even if visually hidden.

### Documentation
The docs at `/docs` use the same tokens: a grouped sidebar (a `<details>` menu on phones), a content column of at most 780px, ink code blocks with a copy button, tinted notes, bordered tables and previous/next links. Docs pages use the nav in its glass state. Outside the lime GitHub buttons, the site links to its own docs, not to GitHub.

### Navigation
A fixed pill that is transparent over the hero and turns to `glass-strong` once scrolled. Brand on the left, mono links in the middle, a lime GitHub button on the right. Links hide under 860px.

### Signature Components
- **Hero carousel**: 21 equal glass cards (the seven product cards, three times) on a 3D cylinder seen from outside, turning once every 70 seconds. The front card is nearest; the sides round away and fade at the edges. It pauses on hover, off-screen and with the motion toggle, and stands still for reduced motion.
- **Fidelity ring**: an amber arc and a rolling percentage.
- **Scan demo**: a browser frame with a lime beam sweeping a mock checkout, and drift badges popping in.
- **Matcher playground**: real OKLab ΔE and px matching, printing the exact CLI line.
- **Terminal**: types the real demo command and prints its real output.
- **Counter**: odometer digits on a spring.
- **Split-flap readout**: an ink tile board at the foot of the hero cycling real facts (React Bits SplitFlapText, on tokens).
- **Scroll-lit statement**: the problem heading's words brighten from faint to ink as they cross the reading line.
- **Radar sweep**: a light-mode sky-blue radar behind the scan demo (React Bits Radar), decorative and paused with the rest of the motion.
- **Dial**: the logo inside a slowly turning astrolabe ring with 72 ticks, crosshairs, an off-center rete, star points and circular lettering.

### Section heads
The hero and the problem statement are centered. Every other section uses a split head: eyebrow and heading on the left, one sentence of context on the right, stacking under 860px.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use tokens for every color, space, radius, size, family and weight, and run `astrolabe check` on the page.
- Show real output and true facts.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, pause motion off-screen, and offer a pause control for looping motion.
- Keep focus visible: ink outline with a lime halo.

### Don't
- Don't add stats, testimonials, client logos or stock photos.
- Don't put glows under buttons, use gradient text, or use pure black.
- Don't use `clamp()` sizes, arbitrary pixel values, or a new accent color.
- Don't add UI libraries (Tailwind, Framer Motion, icon packs). Use plain CSS, the SVG sprite in `components/Icons.jsx`, and small React components.
