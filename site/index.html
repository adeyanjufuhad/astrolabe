How it works

Real browser. Real styles. Every value checked.

  1. 01

    Render

    Launches your installed Chrome or Edge headless and loads the page, fonts and all.

  2. 02

    Read

    Collects the computed styles of every visible element. Hidden nodes and margin: auto are skipped.

  3. 03

    Match

    Compares colors in perceptual OKLab space and lengths in px against your tokens.

  4. 04

    Report

    Prints near-misses first, then off-system values, each with the nearest token.

Near-miss vs off-system

A near-miss is almost a token, probably a typo. Off-system means no close token: a one-off, or a gap in your system.

Get started

Three minutes to your first score

1

Install

Needs Go 1.24 or newer. Prebuilt binaries are on the way.

$ go install github.com/adeyanjufuhad/astrolabe/cmd/astrolabe@latest
2

Check a page

A URL, your dev server or a local HTML file. Uses the Chrome or Edge already on your machine.

$ astrolabe check localhost:3000 --tokens tokens.json --html report.html
3

Guard every pull request

Add the Action. It comments the score and fails below your threshold.

on: pull_request
permissions: { contents: read, pull-requests: write }
jobs:
  astrolabe:
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    steps:
      - uses: actions/checkout@v4
      # ...start your app or wait for a preview deploy...
      - uses: adeyanjufuhad/astrolabe@v0.1.0
        with:
          urls: http://localhost:3000
          tokens: tokens.json
          fail-under: 90
